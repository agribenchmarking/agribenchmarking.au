Cookie policy

A cookie is a short note a website asks your browser to keep. This site does not set any cookies.

What this site does not use

There is no advertising cookie, no analytics cookie, and no social-media cookie. Information is not sold.

Typeface

Pages request the Figtree typeface from Google Fonts. That request goes to Google so the letters can be drawn. This site does not send your name or email with it, and it does not use that request to follow you across other websites.

Other websites

Links to the hazelnut platform, the truffle platform, and partner sites leave this website. Those sites set their own cookies, if any.

Questions

Questions can be sent to agriculture-benchmarking@lists.unimelb.edu.au. See also the privacy statement.

Australian Horticulture Benchmarking Platform

A collaborative record for Australian horticulture. Each industry contributes its own figures and keeps its own benchmarks.
