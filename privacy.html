Privacy

The Australian Horticulture Benchmarking Platform is a public website run by the University of Melbourne. This statement says what this website does with personal information.

The University respects the privacy of anyone who deals with it. It handles personal information under the Privacy and Data Protection Act 2014 (Vic). Where they apply, the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the European Union General Data Protection Regulation also apply. The University’s Privacy Policy (MPF1104) covers every part of the University, including this platform.

Privacy at the University of Melbourne

What this website collects

This overview site does not ask you to create an account, and it does not collect farm, production, or financial figures.

  • If you email agriculture-benchmarking@lists.unimelb.edu.au, the University receives your address and the message you send. That message is used to reply to you and to administer the platform. This page does not add you to a mailing list.
  • If you press Accept on the notice at the bottom of a page, your browser stores one cookie. The cookie policy describes it.

How information is used

Personal information is used for the purpose it was collected, for a related purpose you could reasonably expect, where you have agreed, or where a provider engaged by the University needs it to carry out that work. It is not sold.

The University takes reasonable steps to keep personal information accurate, and to protect it from misuse, loss, and unauthorised access.

Industry sites

Hazelnut and truffle benchmarking are separate websites. A grower who registers there is told, on that site, what is collected and why. This statement does not replace those notices.

Questions

Questions about this website can be sent to agriculture-benchmarking@lists.unimelb.edu.au. A privacy enquiry or complaint to the University can be made from the University privacy page.
